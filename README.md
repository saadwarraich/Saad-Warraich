# Sidewalk Sums

Math and English games for children from age 2 to Grade 7, drawn like chalk on a playground.

- Seven games for school-age players: counting, adding, taking away, making a number, hopping on a number line, finding the missing number, and story sums. Levels run from numbers to 5 with pictures up to adding and taking away within 100.
- Five first games for toddlers: tap to count, how many, which has more, shapes, and big and small.
- Seven number games for older players, from Grade 3 work to Grade 7 work: adding and subtracting (big numbers, decimals, numbers below zero), times, dividing, fractions and percents, number sense, equations, and word problems. A player in Grade 3 or above gets these by themselves.
- Five English games for school-age players: letters, first and last sounds, reading words, spelling, and sentences. Two for toddlers: first words and matching letters. The English games reach Grade 2 work.
- A switch on the home screen chooses Math or English.
- Each player has a grade. Every game starts at the level for that grade, aims one school year ahead of it, and moves up or down with how the player is doing.
- A different lesson every day: two games lead the day's mix, and the pair changes the next day. Questions asked today or the day before do not come back while new ones are left.
- A little harder after each good day: one more question from the next level, marked Challenge, up to three in a round. A very good day moves the game up a level, and two hard days move it down.
- A weekly task: a box fills on each day the day's rounds are done. Enough boxes between Monday and Sunday open a gift that shows the week's surprise: pizza, a burger or bar-b-que.
- Up to eight players, each with their own progress.
- Secret codes, turned on in the grown-up's corner: each player makes their own 4-number code and opens only their own games, and the grown-up's corner has a code of its own. Codes are saved scrambled, never as typed. They are a family lock that keeps brothers and sisters out of each other's games, not strong security.
- A grown-up's corner shows the last seven days and how each game is going. It also sets how many days the weekly task needs, lets a grown-up choose the surprise, and keeps a list of surprises still to give.

## How it runs

- No sign-in. Progress is saved only on the device the game is played on.
- On an iPad or iPhone, open the address in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Played from that icon, it keeps working without internet after the first open.
- To set up players from a link, add `#kids=Name.grade` to the address, with commas between players. Grades: `-3` to `-1` for the years before Kindergarten, `0` for Kindergarten, `1` and up for school grades. Example: `#kids=Sam.1,Ali.0`. The link only works before anyone has played. After that, add players in the grown-up's corner.

## Updating

The live site is at https://saadwarraich.github.io/Saad-Warraich/ and is served from the `gh-pages` branch. Push every change to both `gh-pages` and `main` so the two stay the same. A device picks up the new version the next time the game is opened with internet.

## Files

- `index.html`: the whole game
- `sw.js`: keeps a copy on the device for playing offline
- `icon.png`: the Home Screen icon
